Ohm's Law Quiz

Work out each problem, then use the feedback to diagnose your mistake.

V = I × R

1. A circuit has a voltage of 12 V and a resistance of 4 Ω. What current flows through the circuit?

You need current, I. Rearrange V = I × R so that I is on its own. Then substitute the voltage and resistance, keeping the units consistent.

2. A resistor carries 2 A of current when connected to 10 V. What is its resistance?

You need resistance, R. Start with V = I × R and rearrange it so resistance is isolated before inserting the two given quantities.

3. A device has a resistance of 6 Ω and draws 500 mA. What voltage is across the device?

You need voltage, V. Before calculating, make sure the current is expressed in amperes rather than milliamperes. Then use the form of Ohm's Law that gives voltage directly.
